# JarvisMK

Jarvis is a free voice assistant for Windows, inspired by the one from Iron Man. He controls your PC for you and connects to the apps you use. His memory learns your taste, what you tell him and what he picks up on his own, so he gets better the more you use him.

## Download

Get the newest version here: **[Download Jarvis](https://github.com/Maverixkk/JarvisMK/releases/latest)**

Download `Jarvis Setup.exe` and open it. 

## "Windows protected your PC"

When you open the installer, Windows might show a blue box saying it protected your PC. That's normal for small apps that aren't from a big company. It just means Windows doesn't know who made it.

To install anyway:
1. Click **More info**
2. Click **Run anyway**

Only download Jarvis from this page.
